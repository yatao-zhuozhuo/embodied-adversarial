# Closed-loop manipulation
Inspect the scene and locate the target visually. Use small exploratory actions to understand the image-space effect of the controller. Approach without colliding, align the gripper, close only when the target is between the fingers, and verify grasp by lifting a little. Keep the gripper closed while transporting. Place gently, release, retreat, and inspect. For buttons and doors, align before contact and move in the mechanism's allowed direction. Use shorter action repeats near contact. Maintain a brief episode-local record of useful motions; never reuse a previous episode's scene assumptions.
